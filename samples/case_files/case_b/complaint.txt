Claimant: Fairview Lending Partners
Respondent: Beacon Title Services

Fairview Lending Partners alleges Beacon Title Services failed to disclose a prior lien before closing.
The title commitment was issued on March 2, 2021.
Section 4.2 states the title agent shall identify recorded encumbrances before funding.
Closing occurred on March 18, 2021 and the disputed payoff amount is $94,500.
Beacon Title Services disputes causation and points to Schedule B exceptions.
