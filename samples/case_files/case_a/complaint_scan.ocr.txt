SUPREME COURT OF NEW YORK
Plaintiff: Hudson Valley Development LLC
Defendant: Mercer Construction Group, Inc.

The plaintiff alleges breach of contract arising from renovation services at 18 Morton Street.
The parties signed the master services agreement on October 5, 1984.
Section 8 required Mercer Construction Group, Inc. to provide weekly site reports and written notice of any delay.
Hudson Valley Development LLC contends that no written delay notice was delivered before the October invoice.
